# Schwarzschild's equation

- sum Lambert and Kirchhoff's laws together to get total change in intensity across slab
$$
\text{Schwarzschild's equation: }\frac{dI_{\nu{}}}{dl} = \rho{} k_{\nu{}}(B_{\nu{}}-I_{\nu{}})
$$
