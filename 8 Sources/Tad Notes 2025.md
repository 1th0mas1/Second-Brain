**Thermodynamics and Radiation Reference:**

> [!abstract] Abstract
> The primary objective of this course is: To learn how to use the basic building-blocks of physics to understand phenomena related to climate and weather on Earth and other planets, including the growing catalogue of exoplanets. As a result, the focus of the course is to understand the fundamental physics that governs planetary climate. The course is largely Earth-centric, but these fundamental principles will be taught in a manner that can be generalised to apply to all other planets. These course notes will cover the first fifteen lectures of C5. They are organised as follows: 1. Synopsis of planetary climates, big questions in planetary climate (1 lecture) 2. Thermodynamics of planetary atmospheres, both dry and moist (« 5 lectures) 3. Radiative transfer of planetary atmospheres (« 9 lectures)
