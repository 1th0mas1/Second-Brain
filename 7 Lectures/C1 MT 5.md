### Massive neutrinos in almost any era

	- no strong interactions

	- they will cluster like matter and radiation
	- small they will free stream from one region to another
		- this damping of small scale mode

$$
0= \frac{df}{dt} = \frac{\partial{}f}{\partial{}t}+\underbrace{\frac{ \partial f }{ \partial x^{i} } \frac{ \partial x^{i} }{ \partial t }}_{\text{freee streaming}} +\underbrace{\frac{ \partial f }{ \partial |p| }\frac{ \partial |p| }{ \partial t }  }_{\text{response to gravity}}+\underbrace{\frac{ \partial f }{ \partial \hat{p}^{i} } \frac{ \partial \hat{p}^{i} }{ \partial t } }_{\text{gravitational lensing}}

$$
\frac{ \partial f }{ \partial t } +\frac{p\hat{p}^{i}}{Ea}\frac{ \partial f }{ \partial x_{i} } =0
