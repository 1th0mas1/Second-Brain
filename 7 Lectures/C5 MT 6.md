# Overview

![[Pasted image 20261108155127.png]]
- conservation:
$$
F_{S}+F_{\text{atm, down}} = \sigma{} T_{S}^{4}
$$
- the equation with albedo and $f{}$ factor is for atmosphere, here we absorb this into these terms
- thus the surface temperature is
$$
T_{S} = \left( \frac{F_{S}+F_{\text{atm, down}}}{\sigma{}} \right) ^{1/4}
$$
- without an atmosphere
- ---
- the low pressure region is nearly transparent to infrared coming up from below
	- we say IR absorptivity.emissivity is $\epsilon{}_{IR}\ll 1{}$ and its temperature is $T_{\text{skin}}{}$
- from kirchoff law , it absorbs fraction $\epsilon{}{}_{IR}$ of upwelling flux 
![[Pasted image 20261108163430.png]]
- its energy balance is
$$
\underbrace{\epsilon{}_{IR}\sigma{} T_{e}^{4}}_{\text{absorbed from below}}=\underbrace{ \epsilon{}_{IR}\sigma{}T_{\text{skin}}^{4}}_{\text{emitted upward}}+\underbrace{\epsilon{}_{IR}\sigma{}T_{\text{skin}}^{4}}_{\text{emitted downward}}
$$
- we thus find
$$
T_{\text{skin}} = \frac{T_{e}}{2^{1/4}}
$$
- ---
- now what if consider troposphere lapse rate
![[Pasted image 20261108173935.png]]
$$
T(z) = T_{e}-\Gamma{}(z-z_{\text{surf}})
$$
- this is for $z_{\text{surf}}\leq z \leq z_{\text{trop}}{}$
- over $z_{\text{trop}}{}$ we see
$$
T(z)=T_{\text{skin}} = \frac{T_{e}}{2^{1/4}}
$$
- we know that the temperature profile is continuous
$$
z_{\text{trop}}=z_{\text{surf}}+\frac{T_{e}-T_{\text{skin}}}{\Gamma{}}= z_{\text{surf}}+\frac{T_{e}}{\Gamma{}}(1-2^{-1/4})
$$
